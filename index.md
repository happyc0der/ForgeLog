# ForgeLog

An offline-first Android gym tracker: build programs, run workouts from them, log sets quickly, and
compare sessions over time.

No accounts, no backend, no analytics, no ads. There is no `INTERNET` permission in the app, so your
training data stays on your phone and leaves it only if you export it yourself.

- **[Privacy policy](privacy-policy)** — what the app does with your information, which is nothing
